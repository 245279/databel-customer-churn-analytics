# Databel Customer Churn Analysis & Dashboard

An end-to-end customer retention and churn analysis dashboard built for **Databel**, designed to identify key churn drivers, evaluate customer demographics, and measure the impact of service interactions on customer retention.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

### 1. Primary Churn Reasons & Categories
* **Competitor Impact:** The top driver of customer loss is aggressive competitor offers (303 churned) and superior competitor devices (297 churned).
* **Service & Support:** Support person attitude accounts for 203 churned customers, indicating a crucial area for internal staff training.
* **Pricing & Data:** Uncompetitive data limits and high pricing directly contribute to secondary churn clusters.

### 2. Contract Type & Demographics
* **Contract Duration:** Monthly contract holders exhibit significantly higher churn rates compared to yearly contract holders across all gender demographics.
* **Age Groups:** Churn rates steadily increase in older demographic bins, peaking near the 80–85 age range.

### 3. Customer Service Interactions
* A direct positive correlation exists between the number of customer service calls and overall churn likelihood—customers making frequent service calls represent high-risk accounts.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations Included

1. **KPI Scorecards:** High-level metrics for customer totals, revenue, overall churn rate, and average age.
2. **Top 10 Churn Reasons (Bar Chart):** Granular breakdown of individual churn triggers.
3. **Churn Drivers Matrix (Treemap):** Categorical grouping of churn causes by competitor, attitude, price, and network factors.
4. **Age Distribution vs. Churn Rate (Combo Chart):** Total customer distribution overlaid with age-group churn percentages.
5. **Contract & Gender Breakdowns:** Comparative analysis of monthly vs. yearly retention across demographic segments.
6. **Customer Service Call Sensitivity:** Trendline mapping churn probability against service call frequency.
